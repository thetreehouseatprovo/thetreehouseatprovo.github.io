Hey, I'm Miles

Bringing Provo together through good events, music, and community.

Why I Do This

I firmly believe there aren't enough free, open things to do in Provo that actually let people come together, make real friends, and hang out without pressure. That’s why I host local events—from live concert nights to game nights.

I’m big on music, awesome sound systems, movie nights, dancing, and card games. Basically, I’m just a guy who loves living life to the fullest, helping others, and bringing people together.

Want to meet? I am always down to meet new people! Also go on casual dates sometimes.

Collaborate & Connect

Planning an event? If you’re trying to put together a gathering and need audio gear, DJs, bands, let me know! I am usally happy to connect people.

Want to get involved? I'm always looking for people who want to help plan future events, volunteer, or bring their own ideas. I am always open to improvements.
